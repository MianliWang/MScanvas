## 1. Purpose and the owner's direction

The owner, 2026-10-30 (verbatim): "我们需要把这些后端功能都计划好来做，只有msconvert的其实只能算初版，但是也算是能用的MVP了".

So this roadmap plans and builds every function of the owner's v5.11 demo that MSCanvas lacks, on top of the converter, viewer and figure MVP:

- the analysis pages 特征与注释 (Features), 统计与质控 (Statistics/QC) and 导出与记录 (Export/records), and the analysis steps of 处理工作流 (Workflow);
- the viewer subtabs 扫描浏览 (Scans), 二维特征分布 (2D map) and 元数据 (Metadata).

Analysis runs on two tracks (SYNTHESIS section 6.2). **Track A** needs no Python: an imported feature table, sample metadata and Rust statistics. **Track B** is pyOpenMS feature detection, alignment, gap filling and annotation, and reaches users only through a released runtime.

The owner, 2026-10-30 14:09 UTC, on the redesign (verbatim): "这几个前端选项我都不满意". On 2026-10-30 the owner answered FE-2 as recommended: the target is the owner's own v5.11 demo, ported onto #164's tokens and components, with one real screen shown first and no more theme rounds. Therefore:

- no item applies a theme;
- #164 merges for its behaviour only, as the functional baseline (FE-1);
- the look is applied once by N-02, after that first screen (FE-2), and N-02 gates nothing;
- exported figure colours stay as they are on `main`.

Binding preferences, carried into every item:

- The three most-used jobs come first: batch conversion, viewing spectra and publication figures. They finish in phases 0–2, before any analysis page.
- The interface explains itself: no secondary explanation text. States and labels are values.

- Thermo first, Bruker later. No decompilation or reverse engineering of vendor software.
- No new dependency without the owner's approval. The owner approved `nalgebra` and `statrs` on 2026-10-30 (S-1). The same day the shipped runtime's wheels were approved as production dependencies, effective at the first runtime release (B-1, ADR 0066 question 8). *2026-11-03:* no answer approves a new dependency. matchms and its closure (A-25's runtime, CK-1), `hdf5-reader` (the deep check, CK-8), `parquet` (the export, CK-4), pyOpenMS 3.6.0 with `nanobind-backend` (A-07, B-2.1) and scikit-learn (the direction of S-4.2) are each asked for when the work starts. `ms_entropy` is no longer asked for (B-8.1), and `resvg`'s raster feature is not needed (MAP-1.4). *2026-11-04 (A-03):* pyOpenMS 3.6.0, `nanobind-backend` 1.0.0, the newest CPython 3.14 embeddable and numpy 2.5.3's cp314 wheel are approved in a temporary directory only, for the probe and the AppContainer measurement. ## 2. What exists now: the MVP

MSCanvas 0.1.0-beta.1 was published on 2026-10-28 as an unsigned 6.47 MB Windows installer with a verified build attestation. On `main`, 62 of the 196 v5.11 functions are implemented. The product converts vendor files to mzML in batches through the user's own ProteoWizard `msconvert`, Thermo first, with typed refusals and no overwrite. It views mzML (chromatogram, spectrum and scan table, read through `msaccess`). It exports publication figures and their data, and saves a schema-4 project file. The targeted MS1 analysis (ANA-004, pyOpenMS 3.5.0) runs only on a development runtime. Another 21 functions sit in open PRs, 3 are in progress and 26 are partial. The owner calls this a usable MVP and a first version only.

## 3. How to run it

- **Slice:** one working session, 0.3–1 calendar day (AFTER_BETA1_PLAN section 4).
- **Sizes:** S = 1, M = 2–4, L = 5–8 and XL = 9 or more slices. This is the non-runtime plan's scale; the analysis plan's labels are re-derived from its slice counts. A range such as 4–6 is the source's own range. The critical path uses the upper count and also states the lower.
- **Ids:**
  - N-xx come from `non-runtime.md` and A-xx from `analysis.md`.
  - Merged: the analysis plan's A-01 is N-10, and its A-10 and A-11 are N-F4.
  - Split:
    - A-20, A-21, A-22 and A-25 into an engine item and an -UI item. The interface slices of A-23 and A-24 are A-23-UI.
    - A-08 into its record (A-08) and its code (A-08-KINDS).
    - N-X3 into the precursor map (N-X3) and the MS1 raster (N-X4, new).
- **Depends on:** must have merged first.
- **After:** file order only. The item edits files that an earlier item edits, in the same lane or at a hand-over.
- **Phases:** an item starts as soon as its Depends-on and After items have merged. A phase is not a barrier. It groups items whose lanes own disjoint files, in the order the work becomes possible.
- **Closes:** the v5.11 rows the item makes available to users, each row exactly once.
- **Extends:** a row closed earlier that the item deepens.
- **Interface items** include `en` and `zh-CN` resources, and rendered QA of the loading, empty, error and keyboard states at 960x640 and at a wide viewport.
- **Decision:** a `OWNER_DECISIONS_V511.md` id. Work may start on the recommendation. The gated part merges only after the owner's answer. The 21 decisions of its section 3 were answered on 2026-10-30 (its section 1b), the follow-up questions on 2026-10-31 (its section 1c), and the other 32 with every later question on 2026-11-03 (its section 1d). A row of section 1d names what it leaves open. *2026-11-04:* round two's rows are its section 1e, and each names what it leaves open.
